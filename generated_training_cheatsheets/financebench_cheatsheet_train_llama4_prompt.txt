To determine which activity brought in the most cash flow among operations, investing, and financing activities, review the Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows. (Reference: Current Input)
</description>
<example>
For AMD in FY2022:
1. Identify the net cash provided by operating activities: $1,071 million.
2. Identify the net cash used in investing activities: -$686 million.
3. Identify the net cash used in financing activities: -$3,702 million.
4. Compare the values: Operating activities ($1,071 million), Investing activities (-$686 million), Financing activities (-$3,702 million).
5. Determine which activity brought in the most cash flow: Operating activities.

Example insight:
AMD brought in the most cash flow from operating activities in FY2022.

Example Python code to compare cash flows:
```python
operating_cash_flow = 1071  # in millions
investing_cash_flow = -686  # in millions
financing_cash_flow = -3702  # in millions

cash_flows = {
    "Operating": operating_cash_flow,
    "Investing": investing_cash_flow,
    "Financing": financing_cash_flow
}

max_cash_flow_activity = max(cash_flows, key=cash_flows.get)
print(f"The activity that brought in the most cash flow for AMD in FY2022 was: {max_cash_flow_activity}")
```
