3. Cross-reference with metrics like net card fees or billed business growth to confirm retention success.

American Express 2022:
- Statement: "Card Member retention remained high, demonstrating the impact of investments we have made in our premium value propositions."
- Conclusion: Yes, American Express retained card members during 2022.
