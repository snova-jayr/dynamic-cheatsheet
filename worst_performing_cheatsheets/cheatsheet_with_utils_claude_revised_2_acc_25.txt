## STEP-BY-STEP METHODOLOGY
To determine the correct US GAAP tag for a given entity, follow these steps:
1. **Read the sentence carefully**: Understand the context in which the entity is mentioned, paying attention to the surrounding phrases and keywords.
2. **Identify key phrases and keywords**: Look for phrases that indicate the concept being described, such as financial obligations, guarantees, acquisitions, share-based compensation, or revenue from related parties.
3. **Match the context to a US GAAP tag**: Use the identified keywords and phrases to determine the most specific and appropriate US GAAP tag. Consider the nuances of the context.
4. **Disambiguate similar tags**: When multiple tags seem applicable, distinguish between them based on the specific details provided in the context.
5. **Consider the financial statement context**: Understand whether the entity is related to income statements, balance sheets, or cash flow statements to apply the correct tag.
6. **Prioritize specific context over general categorizations**: When the context explicitly mentions a specific concept (e.g., "exercise price of warrants"), prioritize that over more general categorizations (e.g., "SharePrice").

* **Debt Instruments**:

	+ "stated loan amount" or "note payable" -> DebtInstrumentFaceAmount

	+ "exercise price of warrants" or "exercise price per share" -> ClassOfWarrantOrRightExercisePriceOfWarrantsOrRights1

## TAG MAPPING RULES
* **ClassOfWarrantOrRightExercisePriceOfWarrantsOrRights1**: Use when the context mentions the exercise price of warrants or rights.
* **SaleOfStockPricePerShare**: Use when the context mentions the purchase price per share in a stock sale transaction.
* **SaleOfStockNumberOfSharesIssuedInTransaction**: Use when the context mentions the number of shares issued in a transaction.
* **DebtInstrumentInterestRateStatedPercentage**: Use when the context mentions an interest rate, such as a percentage following a number.
* **DebtInstrumentCarryingAmount**: Use when the context mentions the carrying amount of debt, such as "remaining balance", "balance on the mortgage", "aggregate borrowings", "aggregate principal amount", or "outstanding principal amount".
* **ProceedsFromIssuanceOfCommonStock**: Use when the context mentions proceeds from the issuance of common stock or aggregate proceeds.
